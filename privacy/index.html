Privacy policy

Everything stays on your device

Converses collects none of your data. The people and notes you create never leave your phone and, if you use it, your paired watch.

The data you create

The people, notes, channels and settings you create are stored only on your device. The app does not request internet access, so it has no way to send them anywhere.

What we don't collect

Converses has no:

  • analytics
  • advertising
  • crash reports
  • third-party SDKs
  • accounts or sign-in

Wear OS watch

If you pair a Wear OS watch with the Converses app, the phone sends it a reduced copy of your data directly over the Wear OS data layer between the two devices. Nothing goes through our servers, because we have none, or through any cloud service we control.

  • The copy includes your people (archived ones are left out), their latest notes and whether the app lock is on. Long notes are shortened.
  • The watch keeps the copy encrypted in its own storage, and it requires a screen lock on the watch.
  • Protected people are shared with the watch only by name. Their notes never reach it, except when you approve opening one with your fingerprint or screen lock on the phone, and then they are kept in memory only while you use them.
  • If you dictate a note on the watch, it is sent to the phone and saved there. If the phone is out of reach, it waits on the watch until it is back.

Dictation uses the speech recognition service of your watch, which is provided by the system and has its own privacy policy. The watch app only asks for microphone permission, and has no internet access. If you don't pair a watch, nothing is shared.

Backups

The app's database and settings, on the phone and on the watch, are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. They are not copied to your Google account.

Protected people

If you protect a person, Android verifies your identity with the screen lock or biometrics. The app never receives or stores biometric data.

Exporting

When you export notes, Android's share sheet opens. The data only reaches the app you choose, which applies its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete people and notes inside the app at any time. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, all data is permanently deleted.

Children

Converses is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when payment features are added through Google Play, it will be published on this page with a new date.
