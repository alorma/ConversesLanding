Privacy policy

Your notes stay on your device

The people and notes you create never leave your phone. The app only sends data for Converses Pro and, if you turn them on, for usage statistics, and it never includes what you write.

The data you create

The people, notes, channels and settings you create are stored only on your device. The app never sends them anywhere: it only goes online to check whether you have Converses Pro (see “Converses Pro purchase”) and, if you turn them on, for usage statistics.

Usage statistics (optional)

Converses can send anonymous usage statistics to learn what works and what breaks. They're off by default: nothing is sent until you agree. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy.

If you turn them on, the app sends:

  • the screens and features you use;
  • app errors and crashes;
  • your phone model and the Android and app versions;
  • a random installation ID and, as with any internet connection, your IP address.

Never sent: people, notes, names, custom channels or anything you write. No event says which person it's about.

The statistics are handled by Google through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, and may be processed on servers outside the EU. Google keeps usage data for 2 months and crash reports for 90 days, and handles them under its privacy policy. If you turn them off, sending stops right away and anything not yet sent is deleted.

What we don't collect

Converses has no:

  • advertising
  • accounts or sign-in
  • way for us to read or receive your notes

Converses Pro purchase

Converses Pro is a one-time payment made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your card or payment details.

To know whether you have Pro and to restore the purchase, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a purchase management service. Every time you open the app, even if you haven't bought anything, it connects to RevenueCat and sends:

  • an anonymous, random ID created by RevenueCat, not linked to your name or email;
  • the Converses Pro purchase history that Google Play gives the app (product, date and purchase receipt);
  • basic technical data: the app and Android versions, the store's language and currency and, as with any internet connection, your IP address.

RevenueCat never receives your people, your notes or your contacts, nor any advertising ID. The data is encrypted in transit and RevenueCat handles it under its privacy policy. If you want the purchase record tied to your ID deleted, write to the contact address below.

Backups

The app's database and settings are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. They are not copied to your Google account.

Protected people

If you protect a person, Android verifies your identity with the screen lock or biometrics. The app never receives or stores biometric data.

Exporting

When you export notes, Android's share sheet opens. The data only reaches the app you choose, which applies its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete people and notes inside the app at any time. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, all data is permanently deleted.

Children

Converses is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be published on this page with a new date.
