Eat what you have. Waste less.

Caducity shows what's in your fridge and pantry and what expires soon. No account, no sign-up. Your data stays on your phone.

Features

What you need, nothing more.

hourglass_bottom

What expires

What expires soon, grouped by day, and a notice of what already has.

Add in a few taps

Name, category and date. With batch mode you save a whole shopping trip at once.

Pantry

Browse by category, filter by status or by day, and search what you have.

Calendar

See what expires each day of the month and plan your meals.

  • translateCatalan, Spanish and English

Colour tells you the status.

Four statuses, always the same colour and with the name written out.

ExpiredThe date has passed.
SoonExpires in a few days.
FreshYou have time.
FrozenIn the freezer, kept apart.

Shape tells you the category.

Each category has its own shape and icon. Create your own.

  • eggDairyCookie
  • nutritionFruit & vegFlower
  • set_mealMeat & fishClover
  • groceryPantrySun
  • bakery_diningBakeryCushion
lock

Your data never leaves your phone.

No account, no sign-up. Analytics are off by default and you decide whether to turn them on. Your product names are never sent.
